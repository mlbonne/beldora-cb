## Standard actions

Dash (Speed doubled this turn), Dodge (Advantage on Dexterity saves, attackers at Disadvantage), Disengage, Hide (rolls Stealth against DC 15 and applies Invisible), Help, Search/Study/Influence (pick the skill, rolled), Escape a Grapple (Athletics or Acrobatics), Don or Doff a Shield (AC updates), End Concentration, Ready, Utilize.

## Always applied

Exhaustion (−2 per level on d20 tests, −5 ft. Speed per level), conditions (Grappled/Restrained/Paralyzed/Stunned/Unconscious set Speed to 0; Advantage/Disadvantage from Poisoned, Frightened, Prone, Blinded, Restrained, Invisible; no actions while Incapacitated), equipped items (AC, ability scores, saves, magic weapons, spell DC, speed, resistances), resistances halving damage of that type, temporary forms (AC, Speed, physical scores, saves and skills, the form's own attacks), passive features (Danger Sense, Feral Instinct, Aura of Protection), and the player's own bonuses on AC, Initiative, Speed and Proficiency.
